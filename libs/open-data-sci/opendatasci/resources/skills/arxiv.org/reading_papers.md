# arxiv.org — Reading a Paper

- The abstract page (`/abs/<id>`) is the right first stop, not the PDF — it gives the submission/revision history, subject classification, and often a "Comments" line noting venue acceptance or code availability, all of which help triage relevance before spending time on the full text
- Papers get revised after initial posting; the abstract page's version list (`v1`, `v2`, ...) matters when a claim looks off or superseded — the newest version is usually the one worth citing or trusting
- When a paper claims a new SOTA result, checking whether the authors released code/checkpoints (linked from the abstract page or inferred from the paper's own "Code Availability" section) determines whether the claim is independently reproducible or reported findings
