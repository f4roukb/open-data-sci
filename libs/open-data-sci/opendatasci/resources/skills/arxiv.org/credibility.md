# arxiv.org — Judging Credibility

- arXiv has no peer review — a paper's presence there is not a quality signal by itself; the "Comments" field (e.g. "Accepted at NeurIPS 2026") or an external citation count is a better proxy for validation
- Recent, fast-moving subfields (LLMs, diffusion models) often have the *only* citable writeup for a technique on arXiv well before (or instead of) a peer-reviewed venue — for those areas, treat arXiv as the primary literature rather than a preprint staging area, while still weighting reproducibility (released code/checkpoints) over the claim alone
- A high version count (`v4`, `v5`, ...) without a corresponding venue acceptance in the Comments field can indicate an unresolved dispute or repeated rejection — worth reading the latest version's changes before trusting an early claim
