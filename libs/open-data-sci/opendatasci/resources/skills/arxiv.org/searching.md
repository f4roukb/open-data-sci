# arxiv.org — Searching Effectively

- Search (arxiv.org/search or the advanced search form) supports filtering by category (e.g. `cs.LG`, `stat.ML`, `cs.CL`) and date range — scoping by category first cuts through the volume of daily submissions far more effectively than a bare keyword search
- A paper's listing page shows its primary and cross-listed categories; a paper cross-listed into a category signals it's considered relevant there too, useful when searching adjacent subfields
- The daily/weekly category listing pages (`arxiv.org/list/<category>/recent`) surface brand-new submissions before they're indexed well enough for keyword search to find them — worth checking directly when the goal is "what's new" rather than "what exists"
