# kaggle.com Skill Domain

Curated entry points into kaggle.com — the site's structure changes rarely, but where the highest-signal information lives on it, and which of its parts to trust, is not something general knowledge captures well. Load a skill here when working with a specific Kaggle competition or dataset rather than rediscovering the site's layout by trial and error.

## Navigating a Competition

- skill: kaggle.com::competitions

Where the highest-signal information lives across a competition's Data, Leaderboard, Code, and Discussion tabs, how public vs. private leaderboard scores relate, and where the submission-format contract is documented; load when starting work on a specific Kaggle competition.

## Datasets Catalog

- skill: kaggle.com::datasets

How to vet a standalone Kaggle dataset for quality, licensing, and provenance via its Data Card and usability score before reuse; load when sourcing data from Kaggle's Datasets tab rather than a competition.

## Researching Prior Editions & Similar Competitions

- skill: kaggle.com::prior_editions_research

How to find and use prior seasons or similar competitions' winning approaches — Rules/Overview pages, top-voted Discussion writeups, and top-voted Code notebooks; load early when planning a new competition to compress independent exploration into reading.
