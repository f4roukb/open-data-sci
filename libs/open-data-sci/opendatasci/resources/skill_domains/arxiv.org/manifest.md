# arxiv.org Skill Domain

Curated entry points into arxiv.org — how to search it effectively, how to read what it returns, and how to judge what it returns given that none of it is peer-reviewed. Load a skill here when researching recent literature rather than relying on general knowledge that may predate a paper or miss a revision.

## Searching Effectively

- skill: arxiv.org::searching

How to scope a search by category and date range, use cross-listed categories to find adjacent subfields, and check daily/weekly listing pages for the newest submissions; load before running a broad keyword search.

## Reading a Paper

- skill: arxiv.org::reading_papers

Why the abstract page is the right first stop rather than the PDF, how to read its revision history, and how to check for released code/checkpoints before trusting a result; load when opening a specific paper.

## Judging Credibility

- skill: arxiv.org::credibility

How to weigh a paper's Comments field, citation count, version history, and reproducibility given arXiv's lack of peer review; load before citing or acting on a specific paper's claims.
